## ibrahim
name: Ibrahim
ar: إبراهيم عليه السلام
also: Abraham
except: khadijah, muqawqis, household, wives
role: Prophet, the Friend of Allah; father of Isma'il and of the Arabized Arabs
Ibrahim came from Ur, near Kufah in Iraq, and went on to Harran and then Palestine, which he made the base of his call. By Allah's command he left his wife Hajar and their infant Isma'il in the barren valley of Makkah, beside the Sacred House, where the spring of Zamzam burst forth for them.

He came back to visit them several times. In a dream he was shown himself sacrificing his son, and Allah ransomed Isma'il with a great sacrifice. On a later visit father and son raised the walls of the Ka'bah, and Ibrahim called mankind to make pilgrimage to it. The Arabs kept his religion, the worship of Allah alone, for ages, until the idols came.

## ismail
name: Isma'il
ar: إسماعيل عليه السلام
also: Ishmael
role: Prophet, son of Ibrahim and Hajar; forefather of the 'Adnani Arabs
Brought as an infant to the valley of Makkah with his mother, Isma'il grew up among the tribe of Jurhum, who came to live by Zamzam. He learned Arabic from them and married among them. With his father he built the Ka'bah.

He had twelve sons, though in time only the lines of Nabet and Qidar could be traced. From Qidar came 'Adnan, and from 'Adnan, after many generations, Quraish and the Prophet ﷺ. Isma'il kept the House and ruled Makkah all his life.

## hajar
name: Hajar
ar: هاجر
also: Hagar
role: Mother of Isma'il
Hajar was given to Sarah by the Pharaoh of Egypt, and Sarah gave her to Ibrahim as a wife. She bore him Isma'il. By Allah's command, Ibrahim left her and her baby in the empty valley by the Sacred House with a bag of dates and a skin of water. When these were gone, the spring of Zamzam gushed out for them. The book notes the view of Qadi Sulaiman Mansurpuri that she was not a slave but a daughter of Pharaoh.

## qusai
name: Qusai bin Kilab
ar: قصي بن كلاب
also: Qusai
role: The chief who won Makkah and the Ka'bah for Quraish
Qusai grew up on the borders of Syria and came back to Makkah as a young man. He married the daughter of Hulail, chief of Khuza'ah. After Hulail's death, he took Makkah and the House with Quraish and Kinanah behind him. From about 440 CE, Quraish held them without rival.

He built Dar An-Nadwah, the House of Assembly, beside the Ka'bah, and held every honour of the city: the assembly, the standard, the caravans, the keys of the Ka'bah, and the water and food of the pilgrims. He passed them to his son 'Abd Manaf.

## amr-luhai
name: 'Amr bin Luhai
ar: عمرو بن لحي
also: 'Amr bin Luhai
role: Chief of Khuza'ah who brought idol worship to Makkah
A chief of Khuza'ah, loved by his people for his piety and generosity. In Syria he saw people worshipping idols and took it for a good thing. He brought back the idol Hubal, set it up inside the Ka'bah, and called people to worship it. Most of the rites of idol worship among the Arabs are traced back to him, and the Prophet ﷺ is reported to have said that these practices were first invented by him.

## abrahah
name: Abrahah
ar: أبرهة الحبشي
also: Abrahah
role: Abyssinian ruler of Yemen, leader of the army of the Elephant
An Abyssinian commander who took power in Yemen. He built a great church in San'a' to draw the Arabs' pilgrimage away from Makkah, and when a man of Kinanah defiled it, he marched on the Ka'bah with sixty thousand men and his elephants. At the valley of Muhassir the elephant would not go on, and birds pelted his army with stones of baked clay. He reached San'a' a broken man and died there.

## hashim
name: Hashim bin 'Abd Manaf
ar: هاشم بن عبد مناف
also: Hashim
role: The Prophet's great-grandfather, after whom his family is named
His name was 'Amr. He was called Hashim because he crumbled bread into broth for the pilgrims, whose food and water were in his charge. He was the first to lead Quraish's winter and summer caravans. On a journey he married Salma of Banu An-Najjar in Madinah, and died soon after in Gaza in 497 CE, before their son 'Abdul-Muttalib was born.

## abdul-muttalib
name: 'Abdul-Muttalib
ar: عبد المطلب بن هاشم
also: 'Abdul-Muttalib
role: The Prophet's grandfather, chief of Makkah
His name was Shaibah. He grew up in Madinah until his uncle Al-Muttalib brought him to Makkah, where people took him for his uncle's servant and so called him 'Abdul-Muttalib. He took over the charge of the pilgrims' water and food, and came to be loved and honoured in Makkah.

In a dream he was shown where to dig, and found the lost well of Zamzam. He lived through the Year of the Elephant. He vowed to sacrifice one of his sons, and ransomed 'Abdullah with a hundred camels. He took in his orphaned grandson, loved him more than his own children, and said the boy would have a great standing. He died when the boy was eight.

## abdullah
name: 'Abdullah bin 'Abdul-Muttalib
ar: عبد الله بن عبد المطلب
also: 'Abdullah bin 'Abdul-Muttalib
role: The Prophet's father
The brightest, most chaste and most loved of 'Abdul-Muttalib's sons. The lot fell on him when his father kept his vow, and he was ransomed with a hundred camels. His father married him to Aminah, daughter of Wahb. Soon after, he died in Madinah at twenty-five, most likely two months before his son was born. He left five camels, a few goats, and a servant, Umm Ayman.

## aminah
name: Aminah bint Wahb
ar: آمنة بنت وهب
also: Aminah
role: The Prophet's mother
Daughter of Wahb, chief of Banu Zuhrah, a woman of the noblest descent. She was widowed before her son was born. She said that at his birth a light came from her that lit up the palaces of Syria. When he was six, she took him to visit his father's grave in Madinah, and she died at Al-Abwa' on the way home.

## halimah
name: Halimah As-Sa'diyah
ar: حليمة السعدية
also: Halimah
role: His nurse in the desert of Banu Sa'd
A woman of Banu Sa'd bin Bakr who came to Makkah in a year of drought looking for a baby to nurse. Every nurse had refused the orphan, and she took him so as not to go home empty-handed. From that moment her family's fortunes turned: her milk was plentiful, their old camel gave milk, and their land grew green. She kept him until he was about four or five, and brought him back to his mother after the opening of his chest.

## umm-ayman
name: Umm Ayman
ar: أم أيمن بركة
also: Umm Ayman, Barakah
role: His father's servant, who nursed and cared for him
Barakah, called Umm Ayman, was the servant 'Abdullah left at his death. She went with Aminah and the boy to Madinah, and nursed the Prophet ﷺ. She emigrated to Madinah after the Hijrah with his family.

## abu-talib
name: Abu Talib
ar: أبو طالب بن عبد المطلب
also: Abu Talib
role: His uncle and protector for forty years
The full brother of the Prophet's father. He took his nephew in at eight, put him before his own children, and took him on the trade journey to Syria where Bahirah the monk recognized him. When the Message came, Abu Talib shielded him against all of Quraish. He swore he would never hand him over, and went with his clan into the valley of the boycott for three years.

He never left the religion of his fathers. He died in Rajab of the tenth year of prophethood, a few months before Khadijah, and the Prophet ﷺ called that year the Year of Grief.

## khadijah
name: Khadijah bint Khuwailid
ar: خديجة بنت خويلد
also: Khadijah
role: His first wife, the first to believe; Mother of the Believers
A woman of honour and wealth in Makkah who employed men to trade for her. She sent him to Syria with her goods, and was so moved by what she saw of him that she sought to marry him. She was forty, and the best of the women of Quraish in lineage, wealth and wisdom. He married no one else while she lived. She bore him all his children but Ibrahim.

When the revelation came at Hira', it was she who comforted him and took him to Waraqah. She was the first to believe. For twenty-five years she stood by him with herself and her wealth. She died in Ramadan of the tenth year of prophethood, aged sixty-five. Jibreel brought her greetings of peace from her Lord.

## zaid
name: Zaid bin Harithah
ar: زيد بن حارثة
also: Zaid bin Harithah
role: His freed slave and adopted son
Khadijah gave Zaid to the Prophet ﷺ. When his father and uncle came to take him home, he chose to stay with the Prophet ﷺ, and was called Zaid bin Muhammad until Islam ended adoption. He was among the very first to believe. He walked with the Prophet ﷺ to Ta'if and was wounded shielding him from the stones. He died commanding the army at Mu'tah in the year 8 AH.

## ali
name: 'Ali bin Abi Talib
ar: علي بن أبي طالب
also: 'Ali
role: His cousin, raised in his house; among the first to believe
The son of Abu Talib, who lived with the Prophet ﷺ from childhood and believed on the first day. He brought Abu Dharr to the Prophet ﷺ in secret. On the night of the Hijrah he slept in the Prophet's bed under his green cloak while the assassins waited outside. He stayed three days in Makkah to return what people had left in the Prophet's keeping, then followed him to Quba'.

In Madinah he married Fatimah between Badr and Uhud. He was in every battle: at Badr he killed Al-Walid bin 'Utbah in single combat; at the Trench he went out against 'Amr bin 'Abd Wudd and killed him; at Khaibar the Prophet ﷺ gave him the banner, praying over his sore eyes until they healed, and he killed Marhab. He wrote the treaty of Al-Hudaibiyah. When the army left for Tabuk he stayed to look after the Prophet's family, and in the ninth year he proclaimed the opening of Surat Bara'ah at the Hajj. When the Prophet ﷺ died, it was 'Ali who washed him.

## abu-bakr
name: Abu Bakr As-Siddiq
ar: أبو بكر الصديق
also: Abu Bakr, As-Siddiq
role: His closest friend, companion of the Hijrah, and the first caliph
His friend from before the Message, and the first man outside the Prophet's household to believe. Wealthy, gentle and much visited, he brought many to Islam: 'Uthman, Az-Zubair, 'Abdur-Rahman bin 'Awf, Sa'd and Talhah among them. He bought and freed tortured slaves such as Bilal and 'Amir bin Fuhairah, and was himself beaten almost to death defending the Prophet ﷺ.

He believed the Night Journey without question, and so was called As-Siddiq. He waited months for permission to emigrate with him, and was his companion in the cave of Thawr and on the road to Madinah.

In Madinah he was always at his side. At Badr he rode out with him to scout, and counselled ransom and mercy for the captives. At Uhud he was the first to reach him when he was wounded. For Tabuk he gave everything he had, leaving his family Allah and His Messenger, and in the ninth year he led the Hajj.

In the last illness the Prophet ﷺ spoke of a servant given the choice between this world and what is with Allah, and Abu Bakr alone understood, and wept; every door into the mosque was to be shut but his. He led the people in seventeen prayers. When the Prophet ﷺ died, it was he who steadied them, reciting "Muhammad is no more than a messenger" [3:144], and that day the Muslims gave him their pledge as caliph.

## uthman
name: 'Uthman bin 'Affan
ar: عثمان بن عفان
also: 'Uthman
except: emigrants, thaqif
role: An early believer; husband of the Prophet's daughter Ruqaiyah
Of Banu Umaiyah, he came to Islam through Abu Bakr. His uncle rolled him in a mat of palm leaves and lit a fire beneath him for it. With his wife Ruqaiyah, the Prophet's daughter, he was among the first to emigrate to Abyssinia. The Prophet ﷺ said they were the first to leave their homes for Allah since Ibrahim and Lot.

In Madinah he stayed behind from Badr at the Prophet's word to nurse Ruqaiyah, who was buried the day the news of the victory came, and then he married her sister Umm Kulthum. At Al-Hudaibiyah he was the envoy to Quraish; word spread that he had been killed, and the Companions pledged under the tree, the Prophet ﷺ holding out his own hand for 'Uthman. For the army of Tabuk he gave hundreds of camels and a thousand dinars, and the Prophet ﷺ said that nothing he did after that day could harm him.

## umar
name: 'Umar bin Al-Khattab
ar: عمر بن الخطاب
also: 'Umar
role: Al-Faruq; the most feared man in Makkah, who became Muslim in the sixth year
The Prophet ﷺ prayed that Allah would strengthen Islam with 'Umar or Abu Jahl. 'Umar set out to kill him, sword in hand. Then he found his own sister and her husband reading Surat Ta-Ha, read it himself and was won over. He went to the house by As-Safa and declared his faith.

His Islam changed everything. The Muslims prayed openly at the Ka'bah for the first time, walking there in two rows behind Hamzah and 'Umar. He was called Al-Faruq, the one who divides truth from falsehood. He emigrated to Madinah with twenty of the Companions.

In Madinah he was strong in counsel and quick to anger for Allah. After Badr he advised killing the leaders among the captives, and an ayah came that agreed with him [8:67-68]. At Al-Hudaibiyah he could not bear the terms of the treaty; when the ayahs of the manifest victory came he was overjoyed, and full of regret, and he gave in charity, fasted, prayed and freed slaves to atone for what he had said. For Tabuk he gave half his wealth. When the Prophet ﷺ died he would not believe it, until Abu Bakr recited the ayah and his legs gave way beneath him.

## hamzah
name: Hamzah bin 'Abdul-Muttalib
ar: حمزة بن عبد المطلب
also: Hamzah
role: His uncle; a Muslim from the sixth year, martyred at Uhud
His uncle, and his foster brother too, nursed by the same women. Coming home from hunting, he heard that Abu Jahl had struck and insulted his nephew. He went straight to the Sacred Mosque, struck Abu Jahl with his bow and declared that he followed Muhammad's religion. What began as a man's pride became true faith, and he was a great strength to Islam.

In Madinah he led the first of the expeditions, thirty Emigrants to the coast at Saif Al-Bahr in Ramadan of the first year. At Badr he was one of the three who answered Quraish's call to single combat: he killed Shaibah bin Rabi'ah, and then 'Utbah. At Uhud he cut down every man in his way, until Wahshi, a slave promised his freedom for it, threw a spear at him from hiding. Hind bint 'Utbah mutilated his body. The sight of him grieved the Prophet ﷺ beyond anything, and there was only a cloak too short to shroud him in.

## abbas
name: Al-'Abbas bin 'Abdul-Muttalib
ar: العباس بن عبد المطلب
also: Al-'Abbas
except: aqabah-1, aqabah-naqibs, aqabah-found-out, aqabah-terms
role: His uncle
His uncle, who held the water of the pilgrims when Islam came. Not yet a Muslim, he went with his nephew to the night meeting at Al-'Aqabah. There he told the men of Yathrib to take him only if they would truly defend him. He saved Abu Dharr from a beating by reminding Quraish that their trade road ran through Ghifar.

He marched to Badr with Quraish and was taken captive. Still in Makkah, he sent word to the Prophet ﷺ when Quraish set out for Uhud. As the army of the conquest marched, he met the Prophet ﷺ with his family at Al-Juhfah; at Marr Az-Zahran he brought Abu Sufyan in under his protection, and held him where he could watch the whole army go by. At Hunain, when the Muslims broke, his great voice called them back. The Prophet ﷺ left him the water of the pilgrims, and at the end Al-'Abbas was among those who washed him.

## bilal
name: Bilal bin Rabah
ar: بلال بن رباح
also: Bilal
role: The Abyssinian slave who said "One, One"
A slave of Umaiyah bin Khalaf, who had him dragged through the streets and laid in the noon heat under a great rock to make him leave Islam. He would only say, "One, One." Abu Bakr bought him and set him free. In Madinah he fell sick with its fever and longed in verse for the valleys of Makkah.

At Badr he saw his old torturer Umaiyah bin Khalaf among the captives, and would not let him go. On the day of the conquest of Makkah he climbed onto the roof of the Ka'bah and gave the call to prayer.

## ammar
name: 'Ammar bin Yasir
ar: عمار بن ياسر
also: 'Ammar
role: A freed slave of Makhzum who believed early with his parents
With his father Yasir and his mother Sumaiyah, he was tortured on the burning sand. His parents died for their faith. Under torture he spoke the words they demanded, though his heart never wavered, and Allah revealed that one forced, whose heart is at peace in faith, is excused.

## sumaiyah
name: Sumaiyah
ar: سمية
also: Sumaiyah
role: The first martyr of Islam
Mother of 'Ammar and wife of Yasir. The Prophet ﷺ passed her family under torture and promised them Paradise. She was killed by Abu Jahl himself, and so became the first martyr in Islam.

## khabbab
name: Khabbab bin Al-Aratt
ar: خباب بن الأرت
also: Khabbab
role: An early believer, tortured with fire
A slave of Umm Anmar of Khuza'ah. He was dragged by his hair and laid on burning coals until his back was scarred. It was he who was teaching 'Umar's sister and her husband the Qur'an when 'Umar burst in. Later, worn down, he asked the Prophet ﷺ to pray for help, and was told of believers before him combed with iron combs who did not turn back.

## musab
name: Mus'ab bin 'Umair
ar: مصعب بن عمير
also: Mus'ab
role: The first envoy of Islam, sent to Yathrib
A young man raised in luxury, whose mother starved him and threw him out when he became Muslim. The Prophet ﷺ sent him to Yathrib with the men of the first pledge to teach them the Qur'an. Through him Usaid bin Hudair and Sa'd bin Mu'adh believed, and Islam reached every house of the Helpers but one.

At Badr he carried the flag. At Uhud he carried the standard; when both his hands were cut off he held it against his chest, until Ibn Qami'ah killed him and, because Mus'ab looked like the Prophet ﷺ, cried out that Muhammad was dead. Like Hamzah, he was buried in a cloak too short to cover him.

## jafar
name: Ja'far bin Abi Talib
ar: جعفر بن أبي طالب
also: Ja'far
role: 'Ali's brother, who spoke for the Muslims before the Negus
He emigrated to Abyssinia. When Quraish's envoys asked the Negus to hand the Muslims over, it was he who answered for them: what they had been in ignorance, what the Prophet ﷺ had taught them, and why they had fled. He recited Surat Maryam until the king and his bishops wept.

He came home from Abyssinia as Khaibar fell. At Mu'tah he was second in command: when Zaid fell he took the banner and fought until both his hands were cut off, then held it in his arms until he was killed. He is called Ja'far of the Two Wings, for Allah gave him two wings in Paradise in place of his hands.

## negus
name: The Negus
ar: النجاشي أصحمة
also: Negus, Ashamah
role: Ashamah, the just king of Abyssinia who sheltered the Muslims
A king known to wrong no one, which is why the Prophet ﷺ sent his persecuted followers to him. He heard Ja'far and the recitation of Surat Maryam, said that it and what Jesus brought came from one source, and refused to give the Muslims up. He returned Quraish's gifts, and the Muslims lived in peace in his land for years.

When the Prophet's letter came, he laid it on his eyes, came down from his throne and declared his Islam. At the Prophet's request he married him by proxy to Umm Habibah, and sent Ja'far and the emigrants home. He died in Rajab of the ninth year, and the Prophet ﷺ prayed the funeral prayer for him from afar.

## waraqah
name: Waraqah bin Nawfal
ar: ورقة بن نوفل
also: Waraqah
role: Khadijah's cousin, who confirmed the first revelation
An old blind man who had become a Christian before Islam and copied the scriptures. When Khadijah brought the Prophet ﷺ to him after Hira', he said it was the angel Allah had sent to Moses. He wished he could live to support him when his people drove him out. He died a few days later.

## bahirah
name: Bahirah
ar: بحيرا الراهب
also: Bahirah
except: tribes
role: The monk of Busra who recognized the boy
A monk at Busra, on the road to Syria. When Abu Talib's caravan came, he took the twelve-year-old boy's hand and said he was the master of all people, to be sent as a mercy to the worlds. He knew him by the trees and stones bowing to him, and by the seal of prophethood. He told Abu Talib to take him home, for fear of the Romans and the Jews.

## abu-jahl
name: Abu Jahl
ar: أبو جهل
also: Abu Jahl
role: Of Makhzum; the fiercest enemy of Islam in Makkah
He shamed believers of rank and beat those who had no protection, and he killed Sumaiyah with his own hand. He struck the Prophet ﷺ at As-Safa and plotted to crush his head with a rock as he prostrated. He tried to stop him praying at the Ka'bah and slapped Asma' bint Abi Bakr when she would not reveal where her father was. It was his plan the council of Quraish adopted: to kill the Prophet ﷺ with a young man from every clan.

When the caravan was safe he would not let Quraish turn back, and drove them on to Badr. There two boys of the Helpers ran at him together and struck him down, and 'Abdullah bin Mas'ud found him dying. He lay first among the leaders of Quraish killed that day.

## abu-lahab
name: Abu Lahab
ar: أبو لهب
also: Abu Lahab
role: His uncle and neighbour, and his enemy
His own uncle and next-door neighbour. At As-Safa he cried, "May you perish!", and Surat Al-Masad was revealed. He followed the Prophet ﷺ through the fairs telling the pilgrims he was a liar, threw stones at him, and made his two sons divorce the Prophet's daughters. Alone of Banu Hashim, he sided with Quraish against him in the boycott.

He stayed away from Badr, sending in his place a man who owed him money. When the news of the defeat came he flew into a rage and beat Abu Rafi', a Muslim, until Umm Al-Fadl struck him down; within seven days he was dead.

## umm-jamil
name: Umm Jamil
ar: أم جميل بنت حرب
also: Umm Jamil
role: Abu Lahab's wife, "the carrier of firewood"
The sister of Abu Sufyan and wife of Abu Lahab. She scattered thorns on the paths the Prophet ﷺ walked. When she heard of the surah revealed about her, she came to the Ka'bah with a handful of stones for him, but Allah kept her from seeing him though he sat beside Abu Bakr.

## walid
name: Al-Walid bin Al-Mughirah
ar: الوليد بن المغيرة
also: Al-Walid bin Al-Mughirah
role: Chief of Makhzum, who called the Qur'an "magic"
The elder Quraish turned to when they needed one thing to say against the Prophet ﷺ before the pilgrimage. He rejected soothsayer, madman and poet, and settled on "magic that divides families". The Qur'an speaks of how he thought, plotted and frowned. He was also the first to strike at the old Ka'bah when it was rebuilt.

## utbah
name: 'Utbah bin Rabi'ah
ar: عتبة بن ربيعة
also: 'Utbah
role: A chief of Quraish who offered the Prophet wealth and kingship
He came to the Prophet ﷺ with Quraish's offer of riches, rule or a cure. He heard Surat Fussilat and went back to his friends with a changed face, telling them to leave him be. They said he had been bewitched. After Ta'if, he and his brother Shaibah sent grapes to the Prophet ﷺ in their orchard.

At Badr he spoke for turning back, but Abu Jahl carried the others with him. Then he stepped out between his brother Shaibah and his son Al-Walid to call for single combat, and all three were killed.

## umaiyah
name: Umaiyah bin Khalaf
ar: أمية بن خلف
also: Umaiyah bin Khalaf, Umaiyah
role: Of Jumah; Bilal's master and torturer
He laid Bilal under a rock in the burning sun to make him leave Islam. He slandered the Prophet ﷺ wherever he saw him, and of him "Woe to every slanderer" was revealed. When told the Muslims would kill him, he was so afraid he did not want to leave Makkah.

At Badr his old friend 'Abdur-Rahman bin 'Awf was leading him away as a captive when Bilal saw him and swore he would not escape; the Muslims closed in and killed him and his son.

## uqbah
name: 'Uqbah bin Abi Mu'ait
ar: عقبة بن أبي معيط
also: 'Uqbah bin Abi Mu'ait
role: Who laid a camel's entrails on the Prophet's back
He laid a camel's entrails on the back of the Prophet ﷺ as he prostrated, and once twisted his cloak around his neck to choke him until Abu Bakr pulled him away. At his friend Ubai's urging, he spat in the Prophet's face.

He was taken captive at Badr, and put to death on the way home, at 'Irq Az-Zabyah.

## mutim
name: Al-Mut'im bin 'Adi
ar: المطعم بن عدي
also: Al-Mut'im
role: The idolater who protected the Prophet after Ta'if
Though never a Muslim, he was among the five men who ended the boycott, and it was he who found the sheet eaten away all but the name of Allah. When the Prophet ﷺ came back from Ta'if and no one else would protect him, Al-Mut'im armed his sons and escorted him into Makkah. The Prophet ﷺ never forgot it.

## sawdah
name: Sawdah bint Zam'ah
ar: سودة بنت زمعة
also: Sawdah
role: Mother of the Believers, whom he married after Khadijah
An early believer who emigrated to Abyssinia with her husband As-Sakran, who later died. The Prophet ﷺ married her in Shawwal of the tenth year, the first woman he married after Khadijah. Years later she gave her turn with him to 'Aishah.

## aishah
name: 'Aishah bint Abi Bakr
ar: عائشة بنت أبي بكر
also: 'Aishah
role: Mother of the Believers, daughter of Abu Bakr
Abu Bakr's daughter. Many of the reports of these years come from her: the first revelation in the cave, the Prophet's coming to her father's house at noon on the day of the Hijrah, and the fever of Madinah. She came to Madinah with her father's household.

In Madinah she came to his house in Shawwal, seven months after the Hijrah: the dearest of people to him, and the most learned of women. At Uhud she carried water to the wounded. Coming home from Banu Al-Mustaliq she was left behind looking for a lost necklace, and the hypocrites slandered her, until ayahs came down clearing her [24:11]. He spent his last week in her room and died there, leaning against her, and was buried where he died.

## fatimah
name: Fatimah bint Muhammad
ar: فاطمة بنت محمد
also: Fatimah
role: His youngest daughter; wife of 'Ali, mother of Al-Hasan and Al-Husain
except: umar, first-believers, abdullah, ten-thousand
The youngest daughter of the Prophet ﷺ and Khadijah. She lifted the camel's entrails off her father's back as he prostrated at the Ka'bah. When dust was thrown on his head, she washed it off weeping, and he told her that Allah would protect her father. She was the only one of his children to outlive him, by six months.

In Madinah she married 'Ali between Badr and Uhud, and bore him Al-Hasan, Al-Husain, Zainab and Umm Kulthum. At Uhud she washed the blood from her father's wound while 'Ali poured the water. On his last morning he whispered to her that he would not recover, and she wept; then that she would be the first of his family to follow him, and she laughed.

## ruqaiyah

ar: رقية بنت محمد

role: His daughter, who emigrated to Abyssinia
A daughter of the Prophet ﷺ and Khadijah. Abu Lahab made his son divorce her. She emigrated with her husband 'Uthman bin 'Affan to Abyssinia, among the very first to leave their homes for Allah.

She fell ill in Madinah. 'Uthman stayed behind from Badr at the Prophet's word to nurse her, and she was buried the day the news of the victory came.

## umm-kulthum
name: Umm Kulthum bint Muhammad
ar: أم كلثوم بنت محمد
also: Umm Kulthum
role: His daughter
A daughter of the Prophet ﷺ and Khadijah. Abu Lahab made his son divorce her when the Prophet ﷺ began to preach. She came to Madinah with Sawdah and Fatimah after the Hijrah.

After Ruqaiyah's death she married 'Uthman. She died after Tabuk, and the Prophet ﷺ grieved for her deeply.

## zainab
name: Zainab bint Muhammad
ar: زينب بنت محمد
also: Zainab
role: His eldest daughter
The eldest daughter of the Prophet ﷺ and Khadijah. When the family emigrated, she stayed behind in Makkah with her husband Abul-'As until after the battle of Badr.

After Badr she sent her necklace to ransom her husband; they gave it back and set him free, on condition he let her go to Madinah. As she left Makkah, Habbar drove his spear at her, and in the end she died of it. Later Abul-'As became a Muslim and came to Madinah, and the Prophet ﷺ gave her back to him.

## asad-zurarah
name: As'ad bin Zurarah
ar: أسعد بن زرارة
also: As'ad bin Zurarah, As'ad
role: Of the Khazraj; among the first of Yathrib to believe
One of the six men of the Khazraj who believed at Al-'Aqabah in the eleventh year. He hosted Mus'ab bin 'Umair in Yathrib and went with him from clan to clan. At the Great Pledge he warned his people of what it would cost them, and he was the first to give the Prophet ﷺ his hand. He held the reins of the Prophet's camel when it knelt in Madinah.

## sad-muadh
name: Sa'd bin Mu'adh
ar: سعد بن معاذ
also: Sa'd bin Mu'adh
role: Chief of Banu 'Abdul-Ashhal of the Aws
Chief of his clan in Yathrib. Usaid bin Hudair sent him to Mus'ab, and he too believed. He went back and told his people he would not speak to them until they believed. By evening every man and woman of his clan had become Muslim but one.

Before Badr he answered for the Helpers: lead us on, with the blessing of Allah. He proposed the shelter from which the Prophet ﷺ commanded the battle. At the Trench an arrow cut an artery, and he prayed not to die until his heart was at rest about Banu Quraizah. Their fate was left to him, and the Prophet ﷺ said he had judged with the judgment of Allah. Then his wound opened, and of it he died; the Throne of the Most Merciful shook at his death.

## usaid
name: Usaid bin Hudair
ar: أسيد بن حضير
also: Usaid bin Hudair, Usaid
role: A chief of the Aws, won over by Mus'ab
He came at Mus'ab with his spear, cursing, and was asked only to sit and listen. "That is fair," he said, planting his spear in the sand. He listened and believed. He was one of the twelve leaders chosen at the Great Pledge.

In Madinah he kept watch at the Prophet's door with the two Sa'ds before Uhud, and commanded the Aws in that battle.

## abu-ayyub
name: Abu Ayyub Al-Ansari
ar: أبو أيوب الأنصاري
also: Abu Ayyub
role: His host in Madinah
Of Banu An-Najjar. When the Prophet's camel knelt in Madinah and he asked whose house was nearest, Abu Ayyub said, "Mine, Messenger of Allah: here is my house, and here is my door," and the Prophet ﷺ stayed with him.

## suraqah
name: Suraqah bin Malik
ar: سراقة بن مالك
also: Suraqah
role: The horseman who chased the Hijrah for a hundred camels
Of Banu Mudlij. He rode after the Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr for Quraish's reward. Twice his horse stumbled and sank into the sand, and he knew this man would prevail. He told them Quraish's plans and asked for a written promise of safety, which 'Amir bin Fuhairah wrote for him on leather.

## asma
name: Asma' bint Abi Bakr
ar: أسماء بنت أبي بكر
also: Asma'
except: aqabah-2
role: Abu Bakr's daughter, "she of the two waistbands"
She brought food to the cave of Thawr and tied it up with half of her waistband, and so was called "she of the two waistbands". When Abu Jahl came demanding where her father was, she told him nothing, and he struck her so hard her earring broke.

## amir-fuhairah
name: 'Amir bin Fuhairah
ar: عامر بن فهيرة
also: 'Amir bin Fuhairah
role: Abu Bakr's shepherd on the Hijrah
A slave tortured for his faith and freed by Abu Bakr. While the Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr hid in Thawr, he grazed Abu Bakr's flock nearby and brought them milk each night. He rode with them to Madinah and wrote Suraqah's promise of safety.

## umm-mabad
name: Umm Ma'bad
ar: أم معبد الخزاعية
also: Umm Ma'bad
role: The woman of Khuza'ah whose dry goat gave milk
A generous woman who sat at her tent door to welcome any traveller. On the road of the Hijrah the Prophet ﷺ milked her dry goat in Allah's name and left her vessel full. Her description of him to her husband became one of the best-known in all the reports.

## suhaib
name: Suhaib Ar-Rumi
ar: صهيب الرومي
also: Suhaib
role: Who bought his Hijrah with everything he owned
Quraish would not let him go, saying he had come to them a pauper and grown rich among them. He gave them all his wealth for leave to emigrate. "Suhaib has profited," the Prophet ﷺ said.

## umm-salamah
name: Umm Salamah
ar: أم سلمة
also: Umm Salamah
role: Mother of the Believers; widow of Abu Salamah
Her people kept her back when her husband emigrated, and her little son was taken from her. For a year she wept each day until they relented. Then she set out alone on the road to Yathrib, and 'Uthman bin Talhah led her to Quba', where her husband was.

Abu Salamah died in the fourth year, and the Prophet ﷺ married her that Shawwal. She was with him at Al-Hudaibiyah. When, after the treaty, no one would slaughter their animals, she told him to go out and slaughter his own and have his head shaved without a word; he did, and they all followed.

## abu-dharr
name: Abu Dharr Al-Ghifari
ar: أبو ذر الغفاري
also: Abu Dharr
role: Of Ghifar; declared his faith before Quraish
He came to Makkah to see the new prophet for himself and drank Zamzam for days before 'Ali led him in secret to the Prophet ﷺ. Told to keep his Islam hidden, he went straight to the Sacred Mosque and declared it, and was beaten almost to death, twice.

## tufail
name: At-Tufail bin 'Amr Ad-Dawsi
ar: الطفيل بن عمرو الدوسي
also: At-Tufail
role: Chief and poet of Daws
Warned by Quraish not to listen, he stuffed his ears with cotton, then heard the Prophet ﷺ reciting anyway and believed. He went home and called his people to Islam, and later came to Madinah with seventy or eighty of their households. He fell in the fighting at Al-Yamamah.

## addas
name: 'Addas
ar: عداس
also: 'Addas
role: The Christian servant from Nineveh, at Ta'if
The servant of 'Utbah and Shaibah, who brought grapes to the wounded Prophet ﷺ in their orchard outside Ta'if. When he heard that he knew of Yunus, son of Matta, he kissed his head, hands and feet, and told his masters there was nothing on earth better than this man.

## abu-salamah
name: Abu Salamah
ar: أبو سلمة
also: Abu Salamah
role: The first to emigrate to Yathrib
Among the early believers in Makkah. He emigrated to Yathrib a year before the Great Pledge, the first to go, though his wife's people kept her back and his son was taken from them.

## ibn-ubai
name: 'Abdullah bin Ubai
ar: عبد الله بن أبي ابن سلول
also: Ibn Ubai
role: Chief of the hypocrites of Madinah
He was about to be crowned over both the Aws and the Khazraj when the Prophet ﷺ came, and he never forgave him for the crown he lost. He professed Islam and led the hypocrites. He turned back from Uhud with three hundred men, a third of the army. He promised Banu An-Nadir help that never came [59:11].

On the march against Banu Al-Mustaliq he said that when they got back to Madinah the more honourable would drive out the meaner, and his own son barred his way home until the Prophet ﷺ gave him leave. He spread the slander against 'Aishah. He died after Tabuk, and the Prophet ﷺ prayed over him, though 'Umar tried to stop him.

## miqdad
name: Al-Miqdad bin 'Amr
ar: المقداد بن عمرو
also: Al-Miqdad
role: An early Emigrant, who spoke for them before Badr
On one of the first expeditions he and 'Utbah bin Ghazwan crossed over from Quraish's side to the Muslims. Before Badr, when the Prophet ﷺ asked for counsel, he stood and said they would not say what the Children of Israel said to Musa, "Go, you and your Lord, and fight; we will sit here" [5:24]. At Badr he held the left flank, and at Uhud he stood beside Az-Zubair against Khalid's horse.

## abdur-rahman
name: 'Abdur-Rahman bin 'Awf
ar: عبد الرحمن بن عوف
role: An early believer, brought to Islam by Abu Bakr
One of the five whom Abu Bakr brought to Islam. In Madinah he was made brother to Sa'd bin Ar-Rabi', the richest of the Helpers, who offered him half of all he had; he asked only to be shown the way to the market. At Badr he pointed out Abu Jahl to the two boys who killed him. At Uhud he took more than twenty wounds and was lame ever after. For Tabuk he gave two hundred ounces of silver.

## sad-waqqas
name: Sa'd bin Abi Waqqas
ar: سعد بن أبي وقاص
role: An early believer; the first to shed blood for Islam
One of the five whom Abu Bakr brought to Islam. In Makkah he struck one of those attacking the Muslims and drew the first blood shed in Islam's history. In Madinah he came one night, armed, to guard the Prophet's door; he led an early patrol to Al-Kharrar, and scouted the wells of Badr with 'Ali and Az-Zubair. At Uhud, for a moment, only he and Talhah were left beside the Prophet ﷺ as Quraish closed in.

## zubair
name: Az-Zubair bin Al-'Awwam
ar: الزبير بن العوام
also: Az-Zubair
except: abdullah
role: His cousin, and his disciple
Son of the Prophet's aunt Safiyah, and one of the five whom Abu Bakr brought to Islam. At Uhud, when Quraish's standard-bearer called for single combat, he leapt onto his camel and killed him, and the Prophet ﷺ said that every prophet has a disciple, and his was Az-Zubair. At Khaibar he killed Marhab's brother Yasir. On the day of the conquest he carried the Prophet's banner into the upper town of Makkah, and planted it where the Mosque of the Conquest now stands.

## talhah
name: Talhah bin 'Ubaidullah
ar: طلحة بن عبيد الله
also: Talhah
role: An early believer; his shield at Uhud
One of the five whom Abu Bakr brought to Islam. Before Badr he was sent with Sa'id bin Zaid to watch for the caravan. At Uhud, when only he and Sa'd were left beside the Prophet ﷺ, he fought like eleven men, caught with his hand a blow meant for him and lost his fingers, and crouched so that the Prophet ﷺ could stand on him to climb the rock. "Whoever would see a martyr walking on the earth," the Prophet ﷺ said, "let him look at Talhah"; and Abu Bakr would say that Uhud was all Talhah's day.

## abu-sufyan
name: Abu Sufyan bin Harb
ar: أبو سفيان بن حرب
also: Abu Sufyan
except: ten-thousand
role: Leader of Quraish against Islam, then a Muslim
Of Banu Umaiyah, and the leader of Quraish after Badr. It was his caravan the Muslims went out for at Badr; he slipped past, and sent word that it was safe. He led Quraish at Uhud and cried "Hubal, be exalted!" from the mountain; he brought four thousand men against Madinah at the Trench. Questioned by Heraclius in Syria, he told the truth about the Prophet ﷺ, he said later, only for fear of being called a liar.

His daughter Umm Habibah was the Prophet's wife. When the army of the conquest camped at Marr Az-Zahran, Al-'Abbas brought him in, and he bore witness. He was given a share in the amnesty: whoever took refuge in his house would be safe. At Al-Ji'ranah he was given a hundred camels and forty ounces of gold.

## khalid
name: Khalid bin Al-Walid
ar: خالد بن الوليد
also: Khalid
role: Commander of Quraish's horse at Uhud; a Muslim from the seventh year
Of Makhzum. At Uhud he led Quraish's horse; three times the archers drove him back, and when he saw their hill nearly empty he wheeled round behind the Muslims and turned the day. On the road to Al-Hudaibiyah he rode out to catch them at prayer. In the seventh year he came to Madinah with 'Amr bin Al-'As and accepted Islam.

At Mu'tah, when the three commanders had fallen, the Muslims chose him to lead them. He led the right wing into Makkah at the conquest, pulled down Al-'Uzza, and brought Ukaidir of Dumat al-Jandal to the Prophet ﷺ. But when he killed men of Banu Jadhimah who could not find the words to say they had become Muslims, the Prophet ﷺ declared himself free before Allah of what Khalid had done.

## amr-as
name: 'Amr bin Al-'As
ar: عمرو بن العاص
role: Quraish's envoy to the Negus; a Muslim from the seventh year
Quraish sent him to the Negus with rich gifts to bring the emigrants back, and he failed. In the seventh year he came to Madinah with Khalid and accepted Islam. The Prophet ﷺ sent him with a letter to the two princes of Oman, and gave him command at Dhat As-Salasil, where he kept the command even when Abu 'Ubaidah came with Abu Bakr and 'Umar to reinforce him. He broke the idol Suwa', and was sent to collect the zakah of Banu Fazarah.

## abu-ubaidah
name: Abu 'Ubaidah bin Al-Jarrah
ar: أبو عبيدة بن الجراح
also: Abu 'Ubaidah
role: The trustworthy one of this nation
One of the early believers. At Uhud he ran to the wounded Prophet ﷺ like a bird, second only to Abu Bakr, and begged to be the one to draw the rings of the helmet from his cheek. He led the Army of the Leaves, whose hunger ended when the sea threw up a whale. He brought the reinforcements to Dhat As-Salasil and let 'Amr keep the command rather than quarrel. When the people of Najran asked for a trustworthy man, the Prophet ﷺ sent him: the trustworthy one of this nation.

## abu-dujanah
name: Abu Dujanah
ar: أبو دجانة
also: Simak bin Kharashah
role: The Helper who took the Prophet's sword at Uhud
A proud fighter of the Helpers: when he tied on his red band, everyone knew he meant to fight to the death. At Uhud he asked what was the due of the sword the Prophet ﷺ held out, took it, and cut through Quraish's ranks with it. When the Prophet ﷺ was wounded he stood over him and took the arrows on his back. He was one of two poor Helpers given a share of the land of Banu An-Nadir, and at Khaibar the fort of Ubai fell to him.

## kab-ashraf
name: Ka'b bin Al-Ashraf
ar: كعب بن الأشرف
role: A poet of the Jews of Madinah, who stirred up Quraish
A poet, rich and handsome, of Tai' by his father and Banu An-Nadir by his mother, who hated Islam more than any of the Jews. After Badr he rode to Makkah with verses to stir Quraish to revenge, and told Abu Sufyan their religion was better guided than Muhammad's [4:51]; back in Madinah he made obscene verses about the Muslim women. Muhammad bin Maslamah, with four others of the Aws, killed him at his fort on a moonlit night.

## hind
name: Hind bint 'Utbah
ar: هند بنت عتبة
also: Hind
role: Wife of Abu Sufyan; an enemy, then a Muslim
Daughter of 'Utbah bin Rabi'ah, killed at Badr, and wife of Abu Sufyan. At Uhud she and the women beat their drums behind Quraish's lines; when it was over she mutilated the Muslim dead and chewed Hamzah's liver. After the conquest of Makkah she came veiled among the women to give her pledge, afraid he would know her, and she was forgiven.

## wahshi
name: Wahshi bin Harb
ar: وحشي بن حرب
also: Wahshi
role: The Abyssinian slave who killed Hamzah
A slave of Jubair bin Mut'im, promised his freedom if he killed the Prophet's uncle. At Uhud he hid behind the rocks, waited, and threw his spear at Hamzah: "I killed him only to free myself." After Ta'if he became a Muslim and was forgiven, and with the same spear he killed Musailimah the liar at Al-Yamamah.

## umm-umarah
name: Umm 'Umarah
ar: أم عمارة نسيبة بنت كعب
also: Nusaibah bint Ka'b
role: A woman of the Helpers who fought at Uhud
One of the women who gave their pledge at Al-'Aqabah. At Uhud, when Quraish closed in on the Prophet ﷺ, she fought Ibn Qami'ah and took twelve wounds.

## sad-ubadah
name: Sa'd bin 'Ubadah
ar: سعد بن عبادة
role: A leader of the Khazraj
One of the twelve leaders chosen at the Great Pledge. When Quraish learned of the pledge they caught him and tortured him, until Al-Mut'im bin 'Adi and Al-Harith bin Harb set him free. In Madinah he kept watch at the Prophet's door with Sa'd bin Mu'adh and Usaid. He carried the Helpers' standard against Banu Al-Mustaliq, and their banner on the march into Makkah, until he cried that it was a day of slaughter and the Prophet ﷺ took the banner from him.

## usamah
name: Usamah bin Zaid
ar: أسامة بن زيد
also: Usamah
role: Son of Zaid bin Harithah, dear to the Prophet
He came to Madinah with the Prophet's family. Too young to fight at Uhud, he was sent home. On the day of the conquest he went into the Ka'bah with the Prophet ﷺ and Bilal, and on the Farewell Pilgrimage he rode behind him from 'Arafah to Muzdalifah. In Safar of the eleventh year the Prophet ﷺ gave him command of the army against Rome; when people said he was too young, the Prophet ﷺ answered that he was as fit to lead as his father had been. He helped wash the Prophet ﷺ after his death.

## salman
name: Salman Al-Farisi
ar: سلمان الفارسي
also: Salman
role: The Persian whose advice dug the Trench
When the Confederates marched on Madinah and the Prophet ﷺ called a council, it was his advice they took: in Persia, he said, when they were besieged, they dug a trench.

## hudhaifah
name: Hudhaifah bin Al-Yaman
ar: حذيفة بن اليمان
also: Hudhaifah
role: The keeper of the secret
At Uhud he saw Muslims about to kill his father Al-Yaman in the confusion and cried out that it was his father, but they did not hear; he forgave them and gave away the blood money. On the freezing night the Confederates broke, the Prophet ﷺ sent him across to bring news of them. On the way home from Tabuk, when twelve masked hypocrites came up a mountain pass to kill the Prophet ﷺ, he drove them off; the Prophet ﷺ told him their names, and he was called the keeper of the secret.

## ikrimah
name: 'Ikrimah bin Abi Jahl
ar: عكرمة بن أبي جهل
also: 'Ikrimah
role: Son of Abu Jahl; an enemy, then a Muslim
After Badr he was among those who cried for war. At Uhud he rode with Quraish's horse, and at the Trench he spurred his horse across with 'Amr bin 'Abd Wudd. On the day of the conquest he gathered a band at Al-Khandamah to fight Khalid, and he was among those the Prophet ﷺ forgave.

## hafsah
name: Hafsah bint 'Umar

also: Hafsah

'Umar's daughter, widowed when Khunais bin Hudhafah died between Badr and Uhud. The Prophet ﷺ married her in the third year, binding himself to 'Umar as he had to Abu Bakr through 'Aishah.

## zainab-jahsh
name: Zainab bint Jahsh
ar: زينب بنت جحش
role: Mother of the Believers; his cousin
His cousin, of Banu Asad bin Khuzaimah, married to Zaid bin Harithah, who was then counted the Prophet's son. When Zaid divorced her, Allah commanded the Prophet ﷺ to marry her, in Dhul-Qa'dah of the fifth year, to end the custom that made an adopted son a true son [33:37]. The hypocrites made a scandal of it, and Surat Al-Ahzab answered them.

## juwairiyah
name: Juwairiyah bint Al-Harith
ar: جويرية بنت الحارث
also: Juwairiyah
role: Mother of the Believers, of Banu Al-Mustaliq
Daughter of Al-Harith, chief of Banu Al-Mustaliq, she was taken captive in the sixth year and fell to Thabit bin Qais, who agreed to free her for a price. The Prophet ﷺ paid it and married her, and the Companions set a hundred of her people free, for they were now his in-laws.

## umm-habibah
name: Umm Habibah
ar: أم حبيبة رملة بنت أبي سفيان
also: Ramlah bint Abi Sufyan

Abu Sufyan's daughter. She emigrated to Abyssinia with her husband 'Ubaidullah bin Jahsh, who turned Christian there and died, while she held to Islam. In Muharram of the seventh year the Prophet ﷺ married her by proxy through the Negus. When her father came to Madinah to save the treaty, she folded away the Prophet's bedding so that he, an idolater, would not sit on it.

## safiyah
name: Safiyah bint Huyai

also: Safiyah
except: burial
role: Mother of the Believers, daughter of the chief of Banu An-Nadir
Daughter of Huyai bin Akhtab. As a girl she saw the day the Prophet ﷺ reached Quba', and heard her father and uncle speak of their enmity to him. Her husband Kinanah was put to death at Khaibar and she was taken captive; the Prophet ﷺ freed her and married her.

## maimunah
name: Maimunah bint Al-Harith
ar: ميمونة بنت الحارث
also: Maimunah
role: Mother of the Believers
Sister of Umm Al-Fadl, the wife of Al-'Abbas. Al-'Abbas offered her in marriage to the Prophet ﷺ when he came to Makkah for the 'Umrah of the seventh year, and the wedding was at Sarif.

## mariyah
name: Mariyah the Copt
ar: مارية القبطية
also: Mariyah
role: Mother of his son Ibrahim
One of the two maids whom Al-Muqawqis, ruler of Egypt, sent with his answer to the Prophet's letter. She stayed with the Prophet ﷺ and bore him his son Ibrahim, who died an infant in Madinah in Shawwal of the tenth year.

## huyai
name: Huyai bin Akhtab
ar: حيي بن أخطب
also: Huyai
role: Chief of Banu An-Nadir
When Banu An-Nadir were told to leave Madinah he refused, trusting in promises of help that never came, and went to Khaibar. With the Confederates at the gates of Madinah he went to Ka'b bin Asad of Banu Quraizah and talked him into breaking their treaty with the Muslims, promising to share their fate. When Quraizah fell he was brought in with his hands tied to his neck, declared his enmity to the last, and was put to death. His daughter Safiyah became a Mother of the Believers.

## kab-malik
name: Ka'b bin Malik
ar: كعب بن مالك
role: A Helper; one of the three who stayed behind from Tabuk
He was at the Great Pledge of Al-'Aqabah and told its story. At Uhud, after the cry that the Prophet ﷺ was dead, he was the first to recognise him, and shouted the good news. He was one of three believers who stayed behind from Tabuk with no excuse. They would not lie, and the Muslims were told not to speak to them; on the fiftieth day forgiveness came down for them [9:118].

## adi-hatim
name: 'Adi bin Hatim
ar: عدي بن حاتم
role: Of the tribe of Tai'
When the Prophet's men came to Tai' he fled to Syria, and his sister was taken captive; the Prophet ﷺ freed her and gave her a mount, and she went and found him. He came to Madinah without protection or letter, and the Prophet ﷺ took him into his house, where he knew that he was a prophet, and became a Muslim. He was told that a woman would one day ride alone from Al-Hirah to the Ka'bah fearing no one but Allah, and that Kisra's treasures would be opened; in his old age he said he had seen both.

## heraclius
name: Heraclius
ar: هرقل
role: Emperor of Rome
The Prophet's letter reached him through Dihyah Al-Kalbi and the governor of Busra. He sent for Abu Sufyan, who was trading in Syria, questioned him closely and weighed every answer, and said that if he could reach the Prophet ﷺ he would wash his feet. But he did not believe. His armies, with the Arab tribes who served Rome, met the Muslims at Mu'tah, and news that he was gathering again drew the Muslims to Tabuk.

## hubab
name: Al-Hubab bin Al-Mundhir
ar: الحباب بن المنذر
also: Al-Hubab
role: The Helper whose counsel chose the camp at Badr
At Badr he asked whether the place they had stopped was chosen by revelation, or was a matter of war and planning. Told it was planning, he advised camping at the well nearest the enemy and stopping up the rest, and it was done. At Uhud he led the Khazraj; at Khaibar he again advised moving the camp, and the fort of As-Sa'b fell to him.

## suhail
name: Suhail bin 'Amr
ar: سهيل بن عمرو
also: Suhail
role: Quraish's envoy at Al-Hudaibiyah
A chief of Quraish, taken captive at Badr. Quraish sent him to make peace at Al-Hudaibiyah. He would not have "Ar-Rahman" written, and held the Prophet ﷺ to the terms when his own son Abu Jandal stumbled into the camp in chains. On the day of the conquest he was with the band that gathered at Al-Khandamah to fight.

## zainab-khuzaimah
name: Zainab bint Khuzaimah
ar: زينب بنت خزيمة
role: Mother of the Believers; the mother of the poor
Of the tribe of Hilal bin 'Amir bin Sa'sa'ah, she was called the mother of the poor for her kindness and care for them. Her husband 'Abdullah bin Jahsh was martyred at Uhud, and the Prophet ﷺ married her in the fourth year. She died two or three months later, or eight by another report: one of the two wives who died in his lifetime.
